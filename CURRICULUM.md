| 010 / 01 | Compliance Scope And Control Evidence | 1 | Distinguish a framework, benchmark, policy and evidence record.; Explain assessment scope and shared operating responsibilities.; Relate technical checks to organizational control goals.; Identify why compliance evidence does not prove absence of risk. |
| 020 / 01 | Structured Threat Modelling | 1 | Explain a repeatable asset, data-flow and threat analysis process.; Use STRIDE categories without treating them as an automatic risk score.; Relate proposed mitigations to specific trust-boundary failures.; Describe residual risk and model updates after a design change. |

Every section begins with an introduction. Every module ends with a summary before the next module. YAML checkpoint banks follow selected section summaries; a domain review bank closes the course. They are indexed separately for future application integration.

This course has no labs or playgrounds, as requested by the maintainer. All material is readable without a cluster. Technical content remains draft until review; no runtime execution or lab validation is claimed.
