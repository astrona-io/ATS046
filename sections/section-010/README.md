# Compliance Frameworks

This section teaches compliance scope and control evidence. These ideas help you explain system behavior and choose a suitable approach in a KCSA scenario.

You will work through Compliance Scope And Control Evidence. Each module introduces its goals, teaches the concepts through examples, and closes with a summary. Reusable YAML checkpoints review related objectives after selected section summaries. The course assessment index records their placement and coverage.

## Learning Outcomes

- Distinguish a framework, benchmark, policy and evidence record.
- Explain assessment scope and shared operating responsibilities.
- Relate technical checks to organizational control goals.
- Identify why compliance evidence does not prove absence of risk.
